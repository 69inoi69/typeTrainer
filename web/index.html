help

how the trainer works
  1. You start with a few letters. Every word uses only the letters you have.
  2. Every key is measured: how often you miss it and how long it takes.
  3. Your weakest letter is the focus: it comes up more in the next words.
  4. When every letter you have is typed at {target} wpm with under 5% mistakes, the next one opens.
